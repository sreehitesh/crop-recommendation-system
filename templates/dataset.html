Dataset limitations

What this dataset can and cannot support.

{{ ic.icon('alert', 18) }}
  • No location, district, soil-type or season column exists, so no regional claim can be made.
  • Classes are perfectly balanced at 100 records each, which is convenient for training but unlike real farm surveys.
  • Values look synthetically augmented in places (long decimal tails), so the records are not raw field measurements.
  • Rainfall is documented only as "mm" without a stated accumulation period.
  • Coverage is limited to {{ insights.n_classes }} crops and Indian agro-climatic conditions as described by the publisher.
